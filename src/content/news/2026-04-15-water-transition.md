date: 2026-04-15
author: "Village of Midvale"
excerpt: "What residents need to know about the transition of village water service to Aqua Ohio."
---

In August 2024, the Village of Midvale entered into an Asset Purchase Agreement
with Aqua Ohio. The transition closed in Q1 2025, and Aqua Ohio is now
responsible for water service, billing, and infrastructure repairs in the village.

For details on what changed and who to call for what, please see our
[Utility Transition page](/resources/utility-transition/).
